Our Four Core Products

1. Credit Protection Policy (Credit Life Insurance)
Comprehensive coverage protecting lenders and borrowers through death, total permanent disability, total temporary disability, and income protection benefits.

What It Is
A specialized policy that ensures an outstanding loan is fully repaid in the event of the borrower's death, permanent disability, or involuntary job loss. It is linked to a specific loan — mortgage financing, bank loans, asset financing, or microfinance lending.

How It Works
- Borrower Secures Loan: The Credit Life Insurance policy is linked to the borrower's loan.
- Insurance Activation: In the event of death, disability, or incapacitation, the policy activates.
- Lender Receives Payment: The lender is the primary beneficiary, receiving direct payment to settle the outstanding debt.
- Coverage Adjusts: Insurance coverage decreases proportionally as the loan is repaid.

Value for Lenders (Banks, MFIs, SACCOs)
Mitigates default risk, protects the loan portfolio, and ensures business continuity by transferring credit risk to a stable insurer.

Value for Borrowers
Provides profound peace of mind — ensuring the borrower's family or business is not burdened by debt in a time of crisis, and that pledged assets (such as a home) are preserved.

Premium Rate: 1.8% of the total loan amount (standard rate), paid as a lump sum upfront.
Duration: Matches the loan repayment term.

Key Coverage Areas
- Keyman Death Cover: Pays outstanding loan balance upon death of the borrower. Claims must be reported within 60 days.
- Total Permanent Disability (TPD): Pays outstanding balance when the borrower becomes permanently unable to work. Requires medical certification. 3-month waiting period (waived for accidents).
- Total Temporary Disability (TTD): Covers temporary inability to work for a minimum of 30 days. Pays up to 6 monthly loan installments. 3-month waiting period (waived for accidents). Auto-reinstated after 6-month benefit period.

Additional Protections under Credit Protection Policy
- Fidelity Guarantee: Protection against employee dishonesty and embezzlement.
- Cybercrime Coverage: Protection from data breaches, ransomware, and cyber attacks.
- Business Interruption: Coverage for lost income during operational disruptions.
- Cash-in-Transit & Cash-on-Hand: Protection for cash losses during storage or transport.

Refund Policy
A premium refund is available in the following circumstances:
- Early loan settlement before the agreed repayment term ends.
- If the insurer identifies fraudulent activity or misrepresentation.
- If the contract is terminated before loan disbursement.
Refunds are processed net of administrative costs and applicable taxes. Premium is a true risk premium and does not acquire a surrender value.

2. Performance Security Bonds
A guarantee (surety bond) issued by Lotan on behalf of a contractor (the Principal) to a project owner (the Obligee), assuring that the contractor will fulfill all contractual obligations.

How It Works
If the contractor defaults or fails to perform, the project owner can claim against the bond to recover financial losses up to the bond amount — typically 10% of the contract sum.

Who Needs It
Contractors, construction firms, engineering companies, and service providers tendering for public or private sector projects.

3. Advance Payment Guarantee
A guarantee issued by Lotan to protect an employer's advance payment made to a contractor, typically for large projects requiring upfront capital for materials or mobilization. If the contractor fails to deliver, the employer is refunded the advance.

4. Bid Bonds
A guarantee issued by Lotan Insurance on behalf of a contractor to a project owner, ensuring the contractor will enter into a contract at the bid price if selected. It protects project owners from contractors who bid but fail to execute.

Policy Document — Credit Protection Insurance
Sentinel Insure (the "Company") undertakes to assume and absorb the credit risk under the Insured Loan and to pay the benefits set out in the Schedule of Benefits, subject to the following terms, conditions, and exclusions.

General Exclusions
Sentinel Insure is not obliged to make any payment in respect of any condition or event arising from:
8.1 Intentional self-injury or attempted suicide, or self-injury during the first two years of entry date (whether sane or insane).
8.2 Any act of war, civil war, insurrection, riot, commotion, terrorism, or other acts of violence or military action.
8.3 Any pre-existing physical defect or infirmity of which the Keyman was aware, with an origin prior to the granting of life insurance. This clause applies in the first 6 months of every individual policy.
8.4 Alcoholic intemperance and use of drugs, even when prescribed by a qualified doctor.
8.5 Active participation in mountaineering, horse riding, hunting, motor racing, power boat racing, or fighting (except in self-defence).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of businesses does Lotan serve?
A: Lotan serves a wide range of clients including commercial banks, microfinance institutions (MFIs), SACCOs, mortgage lenders, construction contractors, engineering firms, and corporates in need of surety bonds and credit protection.

Q: How do I get a quote?
A: You can submit an enquiry through our website's "Talk To Us" form on the home page, or contact our team directly. We will get back to you within 24 hours.

Q: What is the premium rate for Credit Life Insurance?
A: The standard premium rate is 1.8% of the total loan amount, paid as a lump sum upfront at the time of loan disbursement.

Q: Is Lotan regulated?
A: Yes. Lotan Insurance Limited is fully licensed and regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Authority of Kenya under Licence No. IRA/05/26054/2026.

Q: What is the claims process?
A: Claims must be reported within 60 days of the triggering event (e.g., death or disability). Required documentation includes medical certification from a recognized practitioner and the relevant loan schedule. Our team guides clients through every step.

Q: Can the credit life policy be cancelled?
A: The policy term matches the loan term. Early settlement of the loan qualifies for a pro-rated premium refund, net of administrative costs and taxes.

Q: Who is covered under a Performance Security Bond?
A: The Obligee (the project owner or employer) is the beneficiary. The Principal (the contractor) is the party whose performance is guaranteed.

Q: What is the typical bond value?
A: Performance Security Bonds are typically set at 10% of the contract sum. Bid Bonds are generally 1-2% of the tender value.
