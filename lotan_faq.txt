Q: What is a Credit Protection Policy?
A: It's a specialized policy ensuring an outstanding loan is fully repaid in the event of the borrower's death, permanent disability, or involuntary job loss. It protects both lenders (mitigating default risk) and borrowers (preserving assets and family security).

Q: What are Performance Security Bonds?
A: These are guarantees provided on behalf of a contractor to a project owner. They assure the owner that the contractor will fulfill all contract terms. If the contractor fails to perform, the owner can claim against the bond to recover losses.

Q: What are Advance Payment Guarantees?
A: These protect an employer's advance payment made to a contractor for project mobilization. If the contractor fails to execute after receiving the advance, the employer can demand repayment of the guaranteed amount.

Q: What are Bid Bonds?
A: Bid Bonds assure a project owner that a bidder is serious and financially capable. They guarantee that if the contractor wins the bid, they will sign the contract at the quoted price.
