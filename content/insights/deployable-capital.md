Insurance-led credit protection is not a compliance checkbox — it is a balance sheet instrument. When structured correctly, it transforms borrower exposure into an insurable credit event, allowing lenders to allocate facilities with reduced risk-weighted asset pressure.

## Strategic application

Enterprise borrowers facing covenant barriers can deploy credit protection to preserve collateral, satisfy lender committees, and unlock incremental debt capacity without diluting equity.
