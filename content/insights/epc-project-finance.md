EPC contractors operate at the intersection of execution risk and capital markets access. Performance security bonds convert contingent completion risk into a bankable guarantee recognised across regional procurement frameworks.

## Lender confidence

International lenders require risk allocation that mirrors Basel-aligned capital parameters. Structured performance instruments satisfy these criteria while avoiding cash collateral traps on mobilization.
